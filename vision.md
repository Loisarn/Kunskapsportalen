# Knowledge Portal

## Vision

Knowledge Portal is an offline-first educational platform designed for children.

The goal is to help children build knowledge through repetition, curiosity, discovery, and long-term retention.

Children should not choose subjects.

Children should choose to play.

The system is responsible for selecting appropriate content based on the child's current knowledge and learning needs.

---

## Long-Term Goals

After using the platform regularly, children should:

- Improve school performance
- Increase general knowledge
- Retain information longer
- Develop curiosity
- Build positive learning habits

---

## Core Principles

### Learn Through Play

Learning should feel fun, rewarding, and engaging.

### Knowledge Before Gamification

XP and achievements support learning.

They never replace learning.

### Curiosity Matters

Discovery and exploration are as important as traditional school subjects.

### Offline First

The platform must work completely offline.

### Parent-Guided Progression

New concepts are introduced by parents before becoming available in normal gameplay.

### Content-Driven Architecture

Educational content is data.

The application should be able to grow through content packages without code changes.
